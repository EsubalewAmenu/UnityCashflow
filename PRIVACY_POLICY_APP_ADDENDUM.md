## About this app

Cashflow Kingdom is a financial education board game operated by Data Science trading PLC. It is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Contact us at datascienceplc@gmail.com about privacy or the app.

## Game progress

The game stores progress and preferences on your device. The financial amounts, investments and professions used in gameplay are simulated. The game does not ask for your bank details or require an online account. Clearing the app's storage removes its local progress and preferences; device backup settings may affect whether data is restored later.

## Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob for banners and optional rewarded advertisements. AdMob can process your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertisements and prevent fraud. An IP address can indicate approximate location. Google's advertising SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS.

Google's processing and retention are described in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [partner-site and app information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your advertising choices

Where required, the app asks for advertising consent. You can revisit available choices using **Ad Privacy Options** in the game. Android also provides advertising-ID controls in device settings. Rewarded advertisements are optional; choosing not to watch one does not prevent ordinary gameplay. An ad reward is only a game benefit, not a real donation or cash payment.

## Support and privacy requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Avoid sending passwords, bank details or other sensitive information. Contact datascienceplc@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of information you have sent us. We use support messages to respond to your request and retain them only as needed to handle it and meet applicable obligations.

## Changes

We will update this notice when the app's data practices change. This notice covers Cashflow Kingdom; the separate website sections apply to activity on our website.
